    Shapes

    name what example
    blend Organic form: SDF primitives (sphere, ellipsoid, capsule, cone, box, torus) blended into ONE closed mesh, in order: union items melt together with a rounded seam of radius; op: subtract carves (a hood opening, a mouth), op: intersect trims. Per item: mirror: x (a pair), shell: t (hollow), rotate, material (faces nearest that item). Meshed on a grid of voxel size, then decimated to triangles. Coordinates are in the part's frame; use origin: keep to place it by them. For characters, creatures, slimes, mushrooms, rocks with soft forms. {type: blend, radius: 0.03, triangles: 3000, items: [{sdf: ellipsoid, center: [0, 0.3, 0], radii: [0.2, 0.25, 0.18]}, {sdf: sphere, center: [0, 0.62, 0.02], radius: 0.17}, {sdf: cone, a: [0.1, 0.75, 0], b: [0.14, 0.9, 0], radius_a: 0.05, radius_b: 0.01, mirror: x}]}
    boolean Boolean of geometry expressions: base (op) each tool, in order. Inputs may carry their own ops, transforms and materials; faces keep their provenance. Each input is centred at the origin before its own translate; give inputs origin: keep to keep the coordinates they were drawn in (a hull and its cavity outlined in one frame line up without offsets). {type: boolean, operation: difference, base: {type: chamfer_box, size: [0.4, 0.3, 0.3], chamfer: 0.02}, tools: [{type: cylinder, radius: 0.08, height: 0.5, rotate: [90, 0, 0], ops: [{type: taper, scale: 0.6}]}]}
    box Axis-aligned box. {type: box, size: [0.4, 0.05, 0.4]}
    capsule Capsule along Y (cylinder with hemispherical ends). {type: capsule, radius: 0.05, height: 0.4, segments: 8}
    chamfer_box Box with every edge chamfered (44 triangles). The workhorse of stylized low-poly props. {type: chamfer_box, size: [0.46, 0.06, 0.44], chamfer: 0.012}
    combine Several geometry expressions kept as separate shells in one part (no boolean). Use when pieces interpenetrate by design and one semantic part is wanted. Items are centred before their translate unless they say origin: keep (then polygon/path coordinates are kept as drawn). {type: combine, items: [{type: box, size: [0.2, 0.2, 0.2]}, {type: sphere, radius: 0.1, translate: [0, 0.15, 0]}]}
    cylinder Cylinder / frustum / cone along Y, optionally with chamfered rims. {type: cylinder, radius: 0.03, height: 0.4, segments: 8}
    extrude Extrude a 2D polygon (XY plane) along Z; supports holes and tapering. Robust via Manifold. {type: extrude, polygon: [[-0.2,0],[0.2,0],[0.15,0.3],[-0.15,0.3]], depth: 0.04, chamfer: 0.005}
    icosphere Geodesic sphere: evenly distributed triangles, ideal base for organic deformation. {type: icosphere, radius: 0.2, subdivisions: 1}
    lathe Revolve a profile of [radius, y] points around Y (barrels, bottles, lamp posts, mugs). Radius 0 closes the end at a pole; otherwise ends are capped. {type: lathe, profile: [[0.2, 0], [0.24, 0.3], [0.2, 0.6]], segments: 12}
    mesh_file Baked / imported / externally generated mesh from a file inside the asset directory (GLB, OBJ, STL, PLY). Keeps authored UVs and vertex colours as attributes. Not parametric. {type: mesh_file, path: source/example.obj}
    random_hull Convex hull of seeded random points on an ellipsoid: the classic low-poly rock/boulder/crystal base. {type: random_hull, size: [0.6, 0.4, 0.5], points: 20, seed: 3}
    revolve Revolve a closed 2D polygon of [radius, y] points around Y (rings, flanges, rims, hollow forms). Unlike lathe, the profile is a closed outline and may stay away from the axis. {type: revolve, polygon: [[0.2, 0], [0.25, 0], [0.25, 0.05], [0.2, 0.05]], segments: 12}
    ring Flat ring / band / washer around Y with a rectangular cross-section (hoops, collars, rims). {type: ring, radius: 0.33, thickness: 0.02, height: 0.05, segments: 12}
    sphere UV sphere (low-poly friendly: few rings). {type: sphere, radius: 0.1, segments: 10, rings: 6}
    strut A beam, brace, rod or post running from one point to another (braces, rafters, rungs, axles, legs). Box section with size, or round with radius. A part whose shape is a strut keeps its asset coordinates (origin: keep), so from/to are where the ends go; they may use params and measure results. {type: strut, from: [-0.4, 0.1, 0], to: [0.4, 0.9, 0], size: [0.08, 0.06], chamfer: 0.008}
    torus Torus around Y (rings, hoops, handles). {type: torus, radius: 0.25, tube: 0.012, segments: 16, sides: 4}
    tube Sweep a regular polygon along a 3D path (pipes, cables, arms, branches, handles). {type: tube, path: [[0,0,0],[0,0.5,0],[0.4,0.5,0]], radius: 0.03, corner_radius: 0.12, sides: 8}

    Ops

    name what example
    bend Bend along axis toward toward by angle degrees (circular arc). Needs vertices along the axis (subdivide first). {type: bend, axis: y, toward: z, angle: -12}
    clean Repair imported/baked geometry: weld coincident vertices, drop zero-area and duplicate faces, make winding consistent (outward for closed shells), optionally fill holes. Kept faces keep their UVs and labels. {type: clean, fill_holes: true}
    decimate Reduce triangles with quadric simplification. {type: decimate, ratio: 0.5}
    flat_bottom Cut everything below a height to create a flat base: a fraction of the local height from the bottom, or at, a y in the part's authoring coordinates. For parts placed by their own coordinates (strut, origin: keep) those are asset coordinates, so at: 0 trims a splayed leg exactly at the ground; for centred parts y = 0 is the part's centre. {type: flat_bottom, at: 0}
    inflate Push vertices along their normals (positive grows, negative shrinks). {type: inflate, amount: 0.005}
    intersect Boolean intersection with a tool geometry. {type: intersect, shape: {type: sphere, radius: 0.3}}
    jitter Seeded random vertex offsets: hand-crafted asymmetry for stylized props. The offset is a function of the vertex position (a fine seeded random field), so coincident vertices stay welded and every OS builds the same shape. {type: jitter, amount: 0.004, seed: 7}
    mirror Mirror the geometry across a local plane and keep both halves in this part (merge: union fuses overlapping halves; combine keeps two shells). For separate named twins use part-level mirror:. {type: mirror, axis: x, at: 0.3, merge: combine}
    noise Smooth seeded displacement along vertex normals (organic lumps, rock surfaces). {type: noise, amount: 0.03, frequency: 5, seed: 2}
    repeat Repeat the geometry inside this part (linear offset or radial around a local axis); copies stay one part. {type: repeat, count: 3, offset: [0.3, 0, 0]}
    rotate Rotate in local space (degrees, X then Y then Z). {type: rotate, angles: [0, 45, 0]}
    scale Scale in local space. {type: scale, factor: [1, 1.2, 1]}
    shear Offset along toward proportionally to position along axis (lean). {type: shear, axis: y, toward: z, amount: -0.04}
    smooth Taubin smoothing (rounds forms without shrinking much). {type: smooth, iterations: 5, strength: 0.5}
    subdivide Split every triangle into four (midpoint; shape unchanged). Use before smooth/noise/bend. {type: subdivide, iterations: 1}
    subtract Boolean difference with a tool geometry (holes, notches, cut-outs). Cut faces keep the tool's provenance/material. {type: subtract, shape: {type: box, size: [0.1, 0.1, 0.2], ops: [{type: taper, scale: 0.5}]}, position: [0, 0.1, 0]}
    taper Scale the cross-section linearly along an axis (1 at the min end, scale at the max end). {type: taper, axis: y, scale: 0.8}
    translate Move in local space (mostly useful inside nested expressions). {type: translate, offset: [0, 0.1, 0]}
    twist Rotate cross-sections progressively around an axis. {type: twist, axis: y, angle: 20}
    union Boolean union with extra geometry (fuses into one closed surface). {type: union, shape: {type: cylinder, radius: 0.05, height: 0.8}, position: [0, 0, 0]}

    Material archetypes

    name what example
    authored Texture set authored elsewhere (written by sw import): the part keeps its own UVs and textures, which pass through to the export unchanged. Not baked into the asset atlas; assign a procedural archetype to re-surface the part instead (docs/IMPORT.md). {archetype: authored, textures: {base_color: source/textures/body_base_color.png}, color: '#ffffff', roughness: 1.0}
    flat Uniform colour and roughness (v0.1 behaviour). {archetype: flat, color: '#8a5a36', roughness: 0.85}
    metal Stylized metal: subtle brushed variation, brighter worn edges, optional rust patches. {archetype: metal, color: '#5a5f66', roughness: 0.5, edge_wear: 0.5, rust: 0.2}
    painted Painted surface over an underlying material colour; worn edges reveal the under colour (chipped paint). {archetype: painted, color: '#3f6b8a', under_color: '#6b4a30', chips: 0.5}
    stone Stylized stone: blotchy tonal variation, darker cavities, lighter edges. {archetype: stone, color: '#8b8f94', variation: 0.4, edge_wear: 0.3}
    vertex Colour per vertex, no texture (Phase 21): the colour goes into the mesh (glTF COLOR_0) with a soft shade toward the bottom of each part and an optional per-part variation. For one-material stylized props on mobile/web profiles: no atlas, no UVs to manage, one draw call. {archetype: vertex, color: '#c98b4a', bottom_shade: 0.3, variation: 0.1}
    wood Stylized wood: growth rings along the part's long axis, streaky grain, optional worn edges. {archetype: wood, color: '#8a5a36', grain_strength: 0.35, grain_scale: 0.03, edge_wear: 0.3}

    Part features

    name what example
    array Named instances: {count, offset} or {count, radial: x|y|z, angle, start, center}. Optional: each: {translate, rotate, scale} evaluated per instance with i (0..n-1) and n (rotate/scale about the part's anchor point; rand(i) gives a repeatable 0..1 value), skip: [indices]. A list of arrays nests (rows x columns): names name_i_j. Radial step = angle/count for 360, else angle/(count-1). array: [{count: 8, offset: [0.2, 0, 0]}, {count: 5, offset: [0, 0.1, 0.15], each: {translate: ["(i % 2) * 0.1", 0, 0]}}]
    mirror x | y | z | {axis, at} | a list of those: twin instances named _left/_right, _bottom/_top, _back/_front. mirror: [x, z]
    origin center (default) | keep. keep: the shape keeps its own coordinates (tube paths, strut ends written in asset coordinates, including measure results); position becomes an offset; no anchor/attach. strut parts keep by default. {shape: {type: tube, radius: 0.02, path: [[0, 0, 0], [0, 0.5, 0], [0.4, 0.5, 0]], corner_radius: 0.1}, origin: keep}
    rotate_about center (default) | anchor: rotate about the unrotated shape's anchor point, which stays where position/attach puts it (a leaf tilting about its base, a lid about its hinge edge). {anchor: bottom, rotate_about: anchor, rotate: [0, 0, 35], position: [0, 0.3, 0]}
    component Instance a sub-assembly from components/NAME.yaml: with sets its public params, materials maps its slots, origin: keep places it by its own origin. Components nest (a component part may be a component instance, 4 levels). Instances accept measure (queries on parts built before), pivot (hinge the group), array, mirror. {component: plank_top, with: {length: 1.2, planks: 3}, materials: {top: oak}, origin: keep, position: [0, 0.45, 0]}
    asset Instance a whole other asset (project or library name, or a relative asset.yaml path), placed by its own origin (or by anchor/attach). with sets ITS own params (pack params are shared, read-only); materials maps its material names; parts are named _. For modular kits: houses from module assets. {asset: house_wall_window, with: {shutters: 0}, rotate: [0, 180, 0], position: [1, 0.48, -2], array: {count: 3, offset: [2, 0, 0]}}
